### 1.3 Lemma REL-G (PROVED, L-RED lane; premises F1, LOC-GATE3, NO-TIE3, (E-DOM), (Core), NP-SHAPE (a))
Statement. Let o relock at t (σ̄_(t−1)(o) = p_e, σ̄_t(o) = p_b), with e_o ∈ E_(t−1)(p_e) (UP_(t−1)). Then Ω_t(p_e) differs from
Ω_(t−1)(p_e) in Λ or in E. Equivalently, there is no γ-only payment.
Proof. Write a := Ω_(t−1)(p_e), c := Ω_t(p_e), b := Ω_t(p_b). GATE-RACE (a) gives a ◁ Ω_(t−1)(p_b) ⊴ b ◁ c, so a ◁ b ◁ c.
Suppose Λ(a) = Λ(c) =: λ and E(a) = E(c).
(i) Λ(b) = λ: otherwise both a ◁ b and b ◁ c are decided by the rewards of λ and Λ(b), in opposite directions. Contradiction.
(ii) Let z be the most relevant vertex of Π(a) Δ Π(c), if any. z is not an e-vertex (E(a) = E(c)), not c_W (never in a Π) and not K0 /
  K3 (with equal Λ, both plays pass them or neither: (Core)). By (E-DOM), z is therefore less relevant than every e-vertex.
(iii) Π(b) agrees with Π(a) on every vertex more relevant than z (on every vertex, if Π(a) = Π(c)). Otherwise the most relevant
  w ∈ Π(a) Δ Π(b) above z is also the most relevant vertex of Π(c) Δ Π(b), on the same side. Then a vs b and c vs b are decided alike,
  and b cannot lie strictly between a and c.
(iv) So E(b) = E(a) ∋ e_o: p_b's play at t passes e_o, hence passes o → p_e (NP-SHAPE (a)). But σ̄_t(o) = p_b. Contradiction. ∎
- Consequence: a NP_(t−1) relock has UP_(t−1) (NP-SHAPE: e_o ∈ E(p_b), with Λ(p_b) = Λ(p_e)), so box 93's open «non-PRIV-before
  relocks» case is closed. The only γ-paid relocks the lemma allows have e_o NOT above Λ(p_e) at t − 1: the K10 «below» case
  (R229-box92 §2). DATA: relG 0 on 29 instances, so these do not occur either.
