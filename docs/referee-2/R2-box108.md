## 1. Items (wording only)
- **S-1 (scope sentence against a citation).** The independence paragraph says the box is written [Q box108] «from the Lead's rule
  (§1.2) and R104's definition (§1.1) only», and Lemma LATCH cites [Q box108] «R104's Fact X: the two β-sets differ». Fact X is a
  statement of R104's packet (docs/red/P-N5-REALIZE.md at trunk 38f4a6a0, l. 66, opened: [Q R104P] «Fact X (used in Lemma KEY). For
  IDEAL-ACYC and two options k ≠ k' of ONE mover x, B_s(x, k) ≠ B_s(x, k')»), not part of the definition. It needs no citation: the
  β-ids are a bijection and the Ξ-graph is acyclic, so β(x, o) lies in B_s(x, o) and not in B_s(x, 1 − o) (the chain of (x, 1 − o)
  never returns to x). With these two lines the box rests on the definition alone, as it says.
- **S-2 (Lemma MOVE, one unstated step).** «level i makes `S5->S6` or `S11->S0`, and e_i moves» needs level i to be a MIDDLE level
  (the top has no e). It is: i ≥ 2 by choice and i < k − 1 in that branch. One clause would say so.
- **S-3 (where SIG may be used).** SIG is stated [Q box108] «At every c_k(t)», not at every state. (F1) uses it at s = c_k(t) and
  (F2) at n = c_k(t + 1) (and n = s in the STOP step), so both uses are inside its scope; the sentence «the wires are at 0 in every
  c_k(t)» covers K3-K5 and «u_0 = 0» covers K0 at n. Nothing to fix; recorded because it is the point where a table proof of this
  kind usually leaks (a signal formula used outside the set of states it was proved on).

## 2. Re-derivation (paper)
Notation of the box. Dictionary to the packet P-N5-EXP: P_j = v ⟺ E_j = 1; K_j = v ⟺ C_j = 1; Q_j = yb ⟺ EY_j = 1; R_j = ypb ⟺
EY′_j = 1; pref_s(x) ≠ s(x) ⟺ x is value-enabled; kmin_n(x) ≠ s(x) ⟺ x is key-enabled in the step; H_i = P_i = 2^(k+1−i) − 1.
- LATCH. A_s(x) is {s(x)} if pref_s(x) = s(x) and {0, 1} otherwise (the two β-sets differ, S-1; all parities 0); s′(x) is the
  key-least element of A_s(x) with the keys under s′ on the lower levels: this is (EQ). Uniqueness: kmin_c(x) reads c only on levels
  below lev(x), so (EQ) determines c level by level. The reading «also when every |A_s(x)| = 1» gives c = s. Correct.
- CMP (a), (b): by DEP the β of a mover of smaller dep outranks every β of larger dep, and the chain of z contains exactly one β of
  dep(z), its own. So for two movers z_0 ≠ z_1 of one dep the symmetric difference of their β-sets is not empty and its top element
  is at the least dep where the two chains carry different options. Correct; the dl values inside one dep class are distinct
  (class 2j: 0..3 for u_j, v_j and 4..7 for yp_j, ypb_j; class 2j − 1: 0..3 for y_j, yb_j and 4, 5 for e_(j−1); class 0: 0..3).
- X0-X7, K0-K6: re-derived from the table of §1.2; they are the packet's V1-V4, K1-K4 (X1 = V2, X4 = V1, X5 = V3, X2 / X3 / X6 = V4;
  K2 = K1, K4 = K2 with K4's enabling, K5 = K3, K1 / K6 = K4). (F1), (F2) follow with SIG: pref(y_i) = Xb(old), pref(yb_i) =
  1 − Xb(old), pref(u_i) = [Q = y], pref(v_i) = [Q = yb], pref(yp_i) = [Q = y], pref(ypb_i) = [Q = yb], pref(e_i) = [R = yp];
  kmin(u_i, v_i, e_i) = Xb(new), kmin(y_i, yb_i) = [E(new) = 1 or Xi(new) = 0] (top: Xi(new)), kmin(yp_i, ypb_i) = [E(new) = 0 or
  Xi(new) = 1].
- The closed form is well defined: for h ≥ 3 the twelve ranges of pos(τ; h) partition [0, 4h + 2] (S0 twice) and none is empty;
  0 ≤ τ_i ≤ 2·H_i = 4·H_(i+1) + 2 for i ≥ 2; level 1 uses t ≤ 2·H_2 (positions S0..S5); the top uses τ_(k−1) ≤ 6 with T6 = T0.
- PHASE: τ_2 = t; if τ_i(t + 1) = τ_i(t) + 1 then τ_(i+1) steps by one unless τ_i(t) + 1 ∈ {H_i, 2·H_i}, where it falls from
  H_i − 1 = 2·H_(i+1) to 0; if τ_i(t + 1) = 0 with τ_i(t) ∈ {0, 2·H_i} then τ_(i+1) is 0 at both times. The three cases exclude one
  another ((a) has τ_i(t + 1) ≥ 1; (b) and (c) differ in τ_i(t)). Correct.
- INPUT. (iii) is read off the positions (u ≠ v at S1-S3, S7-S9; T1, T2, T4, T5; A1-A3). (i): for i ≥ 3 level i − 1 is a middle
  level with parameter H_i, X_(i−1) = 1 on [1, H_i] ∪ [H_(i−1) + 1, H_(i−1) + H_i], and τ_i = τ_(i−1) mod H_(i−1) maps both ranges
  onto [1, H_i] and the rest of [0, 2·H_(i−1)] off it (H_(i−1) and 2·H_(i−1) go to 0). (ii): e_(i+1) = 1 at S6..S11, i.e. on
  [2h′ + 1, 4h′ + 1] = [h, 2h − 1]; u_(k−1) = 1 at T3..T5 = [h, 2h − 1] for h = 3. Correct.
- SIG, by induction on j: aligned pairs give K_j = u (K2) and P_j = u (X4 with (y_j, yb_j) = (0, 1) at S0, S10, S11, T0, A0 and
  (1, 0) at S4, S5, S6, T3, A4); (u_j, v_j) = (0, 1) occurs only for τ_j ∈ [1, H_(j+1)] ⊆ [1, H_j], where X_(j−1) = 1 and K_j =
  K_(j−1) = v; (1, 0) only for τ_j ∈ [H_j + 1, H_j + H_(j+1)], where X_(j−1) = 0 and K_j = v because K_(j−1) = u. Correct.
- Phase tables: all 24 rows recomputed (Q by X1, R by X5, Xb and E by INPUT, Xi by the position); the four starred entries are the
  «both latches on one option» cases and come out as printed.
- The 32 rows: for each row I took the old position, the old columns (Q, R, Xb) and the new columns (Xb, E, Xi), computed pref by
  (F1) and, for the unstable movers, kmin by (F2), and compared the cell; then I compared the moved set of the row with the two
  positions it names (v; yb; y; u and ypb; yp; e; v; y; yb; u and yp; ypb; e for the middle cycle; v; y; u and yb; v; yb; u and y for

- STEP: u_0, v_0 and the wires as the box says; every other mover is in exactly one row. MOVE: level 2 is in case (a) for t < T_k;

- Theorem GK: induction on t with STEP, LATCH, MOVE; at t = T_k the rows `A4 stay (stop)`, `S0 idle`, `T0 idle` give s′ = s, and the
  stop is «stuck» with v_1..v_(k−1) unstable and held. Corollary SW: level i ≥ 2 walks its cycle 2^(i−2) times, each mover moves
  twice per cycle; 1 + 4 + 7·(2^(k−2) − 2) + 4·2^(k−2) = 11·2^(k−2) − 9. Both equal Theorem EXP-IDEAL (b), (c).

## 4. The two derivations side by side
- Same run, same numbers: H_i of the box = P_i of the packet; one cycle S0 → … → S11 → S0 of a middle level is two RESPONSES of
  the packet's unit (from rest 0: S0..S6, the steps v, F, B, u + F′, B′, e; from rest 1: S6..S0); S0 = REST_i(0), S6 = REST_i(1);
  T0, T3 the same for the top unit; INPUT (i) «X_(i−1) = 1 iff τ_i ∈ [1, H_i]» is the packet's «the input of unit i is (1, 1)
  during the first response and (0, 0) during the second», and SIG is «both bits of the input are equal».
- Different structure. The packet proves a statement about ONE unit under a hypothesis on its input, by downward induction on the
  unit, and that induction needed the cut form (G-1 of my review: the hypothesis of the inner application is produced by the outer
  unit step by step). Box 108 has no such induction and no input hypothesis: it writes the whole state at every t, proves that
  the inputs are functions of the phases (INPUT, SIG), and checks R104's step once for each of 32 classes. Its induction is on t
  only (Theorem GK), so the circularity that produced G-1 cannot occur in it; what it must get right instead is the table and the
  class rule, which is what §2 and §3 above check.
- What each gives that the other does not: the packet's lemma (in its cut form) describes a unit under ANY input that starts a
  response, also a truncated one; the box gives the state at every t in closed form and the list of orders actually used (§7).
  For Theorem EXP-IDEAL (b), (c) either suffices.
