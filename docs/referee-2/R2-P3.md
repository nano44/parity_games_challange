- K5'' (repaired; PROVED, reading). Let Dep(Str) be the least set D ⊇ Str ∩ A_< such that a vertex v ∈ A_< \ Reg is in D

  - a non-straddling t' whose escapes all lie in Reg, and with no old vertex in A_< \ Reg, has two possibilities:

  Str ≠ ∅ means a tangle with a vertex in a higher region fired in the rescue loop at a level < pr(τ_k).
