## 1. PAIR-HIGH against ALT-H
Proof check of PAIR-HIGH:
- (a): between consecutive y-tops, y leaves at a change whose top z is above y (E-MON3: the top enters). That change alters E ∩ U_y; a y-top
  alters nothing in U_y. E ∩ U_y is lex-increasing: a change topped in U_y raises it, any other change leaves it fixed. So its values are
  distinct, which gives c_y ≤ Dhi_y. Correct.
- (c) restates the same distinctness: Dhi_y = 1 + #(changes topped in U_y). Correct.
- (b): ⌊L/2⌋ ≤ c_y. Correct. The pair's more relevant letter is used; I checked alternations that start with either letter.

Comparison:
- ALT-H (iii) says alt(x, y) ≤ 2·(1 + H_w) + 1, with w the more relevant letter and H_w = #tops above w. By (c), 1 + H_w = Dhi_w, so
  (iii) IS (b): the same counters and the same bound.
- ALT-H (ii) says c_y ≤ 1 + #(tops above y strictly between the first and the last y-top). The right-hand side is at most Dhi_y, so (ii)
  implies (a). The two are equal unless tops above y occur before the first or after the last y-top.
- Neither form is a polynomial bound. Both were derived independently: ALT-H in R227-hold §1, before I read box 198.

## 2. RA-PATHS
- Each of the c_y − 1 gaps between consecutive y-tops contains a top of its most relevant letter z, and z ∈ RA(y) is above y (a). The gaps
  are disjoint, so c_y − 1 ≤ Σ_(z ∈ RA(y)) c_z. Induction from the top letter down gives c_y ≤ P(y). Correct.
- Small point (no effect): RA(y) is a set. If two gaps share one letter z, the bound still holds, since z has at least two tops then.
- BIN tightness (P = 2^(M − j) = c): consistent with R225-core §2.

## 5. RA depth alone is cheap (PROVED, lane: a sequence; realization in the game OPEN)
- The staircase z_(d−1) z_d z_(d−2) z_(d−1) z_(d−3) z_(d−2) … y z_1 y (letters y ≺ z_1 ≺ … ≺ z_d) is lex-legal.
  - Every re-entry of a letter is preceded, after that letter's previous top, by a top above it.
  - It has RA(y) = {z_1}, RA(z_j) = {z_(j+1)}, depth d + 1, fan-in 1, c = 2 for every letter except z_d, and P(y) = d + 1.
- So depth by itself bounds nothing useful and threatens nothing: with fan-in 1, C ≤ depth ≤ M (box 198 §2).
- The quantity that C-EQUIV and RA-PATHS need is the paths count P (fan-in × depth), or C itself.
- CONJECTURE RA-LOG (depth O(log(N + M))) would be broken by a linear-depth staircase with fan-in 1, but that would NOT break ALT-POLY.
- The data have fan-in ≤ 4 and depth ≤ 4, so P ≤ 8: nothing large yet.
