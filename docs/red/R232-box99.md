## 3. SCC-LIN (c)
### 3.1 ONE-CROSS transfers to Γ3 (PROVED, lane)
- ONE-CROSS3. On HOW3(G) (= N5′-xi's mover phase until its first fallback, REACH3-ALL), if one face (pe_g or pβ_g) of a gate g has a
  constant value over the run, σ̄_t(g) changes at most once, and only toward that face.
  - Proof: R231-onecross §2 verbatim, with F1 on HOW3 (R125: «F1 from RESULTS R15 (a)»), LOC-GATE3 and NO-TIE3 (R125 l. 15595) in place
    of F1, LOC-GATE, NO-TIE. ∎
- Corollary INT-ONLY (PROVED, lane; for SCC-LIN). Let C be an SCC of D(G) and t0(C) as in SCC-RED.
  - Statement: on the states t ≥ t0(C), a gate of a mover of C whose W-target or Ξ-target is not in C (a mover outside C, or −1 / X) flips
    at most once.
  - Proof: that face's play stays outside C (SCC), where every mover is final from t0 on and Odd's reply there is fixed by Even's fixed
    strategy. So the face's value is constant on t ≥ t0; apply ONE-CROSS3 to G_C's run. ∎
  - So inside one SCC with constant exits, only INTERNAL gates (both targets in C) can flip more than once. SCC-LIN has to bound the
    mover switches driven by internal gates.
- DATA (oc3_232.py, own engine; set: MWR4_2, MWR8_2, the 7 C98, MW8_2, Ku_8, C6G8_n54, k1fail_202, WC4_2; unit gates, 1,089; out/oc3_232.out):
  - xfail (constant face, ≥ 2 changes) 0, dfail (change away from the constant face) 0, tie 0.
  - sfail (gate with an outside target, ≥ 2 changes after t0(C)) 0, over 898 such gates.
  - Internal gates with ≥ 2 changes after t0: 94.
  - Control OC_CTRL=1 (flips counted from state 0 instead of t0): sfail 5 (fires; out/oc3_232_ctrl.out).
