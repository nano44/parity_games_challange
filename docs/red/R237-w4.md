## 1. Reading of (W4)'s proof, sentence by sentence (READING)
### 1.1 W-1
- The sentence: «Under σ a pe-free play from a vertex outside the core runs along the 6-edge paths from vertex of H to vertex of H
  forever (every valve goes to y), so it never meets c_W or the core, and its cycle node is a vertex of H (W1), of rank below c_W.»
- As written it is FALSE for exactly four kinds of start outside the core: a pad pe_(v,w) and a pad pe_o (one successor, c_W: the play
  is pe → c_W, value (c_W, ∅, 1)); c_W itself (its loop, (c_W, ∅, 0)); a pad pb_(a,w) (one successor K0: the play enters the core,
  value (K1, {K0, K3}, 6) in a standard state). Pe-freeness constrains Odd's choices only; these four starts are Even pads (or c_W) whose
  only edge leaves the 6-edge paths.
- It is TRUE for every other start outside the core: v ∈ V, and the inner vertices m, g, pβ, b, y of an Even edge's path and x1, x2, x3,
  b, y of an Odd edge's path (σ: every valve plays y; pe-free: every gate plays pβ, every o plays some x1).
- The proof uses the sentence only at: (a) an Odd vertex u outside the core, i.e. a gate g or an o ∈ V_O (both in the TRUE set);
  (b) at v ∈ V; (c) at a pad m (its play is m → g → pβ → b → y → w). No step uses it at pe, pe_o, pb or c_W.
- Repair (one phrase): «from a vertex of H or an inner vertex m, g, pβ, b, y, x1, x2, x3 of a 6-edge path». With it, (a)-(d) read as
  proved. GAP class: wording; no statement changes.
- DATA: w4x tests the value of EVERY vertex outside H against the value that follows from (b), including the four exceptional kinds
  (pe, pe_o → (c_W, ∅, 1); c_W → (c_W, ∅, 0); pb → (K1, {K0, K3}, 6); the core). «W-1 starts» in the tables counts the (state,
  exceptional start) pairs tested: 0 failures on 8,937,520 such pairs in the exhaustive n = 3 run alone (§2).
### 1.2 The other sentences, each tried as an adversary (READING; DATA where a counter exists)
- (a) «a pe-free strategy gives u a value with a node in H, strictly ◁-smaller»: SPEC's order compares rewards first; reward(c_W) =
  rank(c_W) + 1 > the reward of every vertex of H, odd or even, because Ω(c_W) = top is even and above every Ω(V) + 6 (R_W). So the
  comparison never reaches P or the length: true for odd and even H-cycles alike (the even case with a large reward was the
  predecessor's first candidate; it cannot be bigger than top). Counter w4a.
- (a) needs a pe-free option at every Odd vertex outside the core: a gate has pβ, an o ∈ V_O has x1 of any H-successor (TARGET.md §1:
  every vertex has a successor). K2 is the only Odd vertex of the core and is excluded by (a)'s statement. Counter w4a (u ≠ K2).
- (b) «correspond one to one»: a pe-free strategy is forced at gates (pβ) and free at o (one x1 per H-successor): a bijection with the
  positional ρ of H. True.
- (b) «(c, P, 6ℓ)»: the cycle of the stretched play is the stretched H-cycle; its top-ranked vertex is the H-cycle node (inner vertices
  have Ω ≤ 2 < 6 ≤ every Ω(V)); P collects path vertices ranked above the cycle node, and inner vertices never are. Lengths: every H-edge
  is exactly 6 W-edges for BOTH kinds of edge (Even: m, g, pβ, b, y; Odd: x1, x2, x3, b, y). Counters w4b (v ∈ V) and w4x.
- (b) «strictly order-preserving»: reward and P comparisons use the rank order restricted to V, which is H's (R_W); the length
  comparison is a sign test on ℓ − ℓ′ (direction by the parity of the cycle node), unchanged by ×6. Aimed families plen (P empty, the
  length decides every comparison) and pset (P decides) in §2: 0.
- (b) «σ̄ … minimises at every vertex over all Odd strategies, so its ρ minimises at every vertex of H»: a strategy that takes pe
  anywhere on the play from v gives v node c_W (worst for Odd); taking pe off that play does not change v's value. So the minimum
  over all Odd strategies equals the minimum over pe-free ones, and by the bijection it is H's minimum ×6. Uses 1E-N3 (premise; L42 /
  L45). «the pointwise minimum is unique as a value»: a pointwise minimum of values is one value per vertex. Aimed family eqprio
  (one or two priority values; ranks by index only): 0.
- (c) «five more steps, no new P-element»: m → g → pβ → b → y → w; all five rank below w's cycle node. Counter w4c.
- (d) S2's Iσ is «strictly better successor»; v ∈ V_E's successors are exactly its pads. Counters w4d, w4ord (every pair of pads).
- Where the proof leans on landed premises (1E-N3 for σ̄'s pointwise optimality; R117's core facts K3 (a)-(d), R118 K2-r), I did not
  re-prove them; their consequences on W are tested by w4x (core values) and w6core.
