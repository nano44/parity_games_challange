## 1. The R53 counter H_k under R2 (experiments/red/kl/r2/)
- H_k (R53 §5; 4·n^{k+1} dual pivots under every switch-all-type rule) under R2, seeds 1..200 (r2dist.py, hk_r2_distribution.txt):
  H_0 (n 13) mean 14.70, sd 11.68, median 11, max 81; H_2 (n 17) mean 14.73, sd 11.85, median 11, max 87. The two histograms are identical
  except the largest value: the number of laps is random and independent of the counter's range n^{k+1}.
- Why (trace klmin_n13_r2_seed1_trace.txt, ktrace_r2.py): switch-all moves nodes 3, 4, 8 together; under R2 node 3 moves alone, the
  candidates become {4, 7, 8}, and picking 7 closes the odd cycle of G_τ, so the least fixed point sends every label to ⊤ (iteration 5).
  A lap survives only if the random picks come in a good order, which happens with a probability bounded away from 1 per period, so the
  number of laps is (roughly geometrically) bounded in expectation. Law (DATA, 200 seeds × 2 sizes): E[R2 pivots on H_k] ≈ 14.7, constant in k.

## 4. Status at box end (reading)
- No kill of N8d-R2: no incorrect output (every run verified), and no family with mean pivots provably or clearly super-polynomial.
- Obstruction met by the R53 route: the lapping counter needs simultaneous switching; under R2 a cycle-closing node is a candidate in
  every period and is picked with probability bounded away from 0, so the expected number of laps is O(1) (H_k: mean 14.7, constant in k).
  A counter robust to R2 would need a period in which EVERY candidate keeps the cycle open (for instance a forced chain with one candidate
  per step); min-over-seeds climbs (~30 min in all) did not find one at n ≤ 18.
- Open, with the cheapest next step: switchbestexp under R2 is the only family with sustained growth (ratios 1.33-1.64 per +2 N up to
  N 16, best fit ≈ n^2).
  Deciding polynomial vs exponential needs N 18-22 with ≥ 5 seeds, i.e. a faster kl implementation (the per-pivot cost dominates), or a
  structural port of Friedmann's Random-Edge counter (Thm 4.76) to random-node / best-arc selection with tree labels.
