## 1. The R53 counter H_k under R2 (experiments/red/kl/r2/)
- H_k (R53 §5; 4·n^{k+1} dual pivots under every switch-all-type rule) under R2, seeds 1..200 (r2dist.py, hk_r2_distribution.txt):
  H_0 (n 13) mean 14.70, sd 11.68, median 11, max 81; H_2 (n 17) mean 14.73, sd 11.85, median 11, max 87. The two histograms are identical
  except the largest value: the number of laps is random and independent of the counter's range n^{k+1}.
- Why (trace klmin_n13_r2_seed1_trace.txt, ktrace_r2.py): switch-all moves nodes 3, 4, 8 together; under R2 node 3 moves alone, the
  candidates become {4, 7, 8}, and picking 7 closes the odd cycle of G_τ, so the least fixed point sends every label to ⊤ (iteration 5).
  A lap survives only if the random picks come in a good order, which happens with a probability bounded away from 1 per period, so the
  number of laps is (roughly geometrically) bounded in expectation. Law (DATA, 200 seeds × 2 sizes): E[R2 pivots on H_k] ≈ 14.7, constant in k.
