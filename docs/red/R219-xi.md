## 0. Answers
- (b) Lemma GATE-REARM (§3, PROVED-CONDITIONAL on [LOC]). Every flip of a gate g of level ≥ 1 under N5′-xi on Γ3(G) (= Howard on
  IDEAL-CYC(G) until the first fallback, RESULTS R118 REACH3-CYC) is paid by exactly one of three things:
  - (i) a change of the cycle node Λ of one of g's faces. There are at most 4M + 4 of these in the whole run (PROVED).
  - (ii) a RET stretch: the β-play of g passes g and then e_g. ρ_g stretches; each costs ≤ 2 flips.
  - (iii) an e-set overtake: strict lex increases of E(y) and of E(x′), alternately, where y is g's W-target and x′ its Ξ-target. Per
    interval with both faces' Λ fixed and equal, there are at most 2·min(D_E(y), D_E(x′)) − 1 flips.
  Lemma FLIP-E (§2) is the key step: between RET stretches, a gate's face is decided by e-sets alone, never by β's.
- Cost of (iii) (§4). If the plays of y and x′ have no ascent: D_E ≤ 1 + M(M + 1)/2 (L-NEW's DESC-POLY; PROVED, lane). With at most A
  ascents per chain: CONJECTURE ASC-POLY(A). With unboundedly many ascents: OPEN.
- Corollary GATE-POLY (§5, PROVED-CONDITIONAL). If the plays of y and x′ are descending at every state, then
  F_g ≤ f(M) + 2ρ_g + H_g with f(M) = (4M + 5)(M² + M + 1) + 4M + 4 (cubic in M). H_g counts flips in intervals whose shared cycle node
  lies above e_g (DATA 0 in every run). On NOUP genomes this holds at every gate.
  - The asked form «flips at one gate ≤ f(d)» is DEAD: LEAP(k) has d = 2 and 2k flips at one gate (R217 §3, LEAP_k64: 128).
- Open cases: (iii) with unboundedly many ascents (H1-type plays), and (ii) at SET-type gates. There ρ_g is the number of times a bit
  sets: the counter question itself.
- (a) (§6). ASC-GATE cases (ii) and (iii) are both locks: Lemma LOCK-(ii/iii), PROVED-CONDITIONAL on [LOC]. A gate in case (ii) or (iii)
  is released only when a mover on one of its two plays switches (PLAY-BLIND). The release is then an e-set overtake, i.e. GATE-REARM
  case (iii).
  - So (ii) and (iii) are not a new mechanism. They are allowed: no branch of the R218 trilemma excludes them, because they occur on
    ascents of arbitrary genomes, not on hub chains.
  - DATA: a release in case (ii) or (iii) happens at most 2 times per instance in 37 runs, and is followed by a return to β (a re-arm) at
    most once per gate.
  - Smallest instance with a case-(ii) release AND a re-arm: L-NEW's rd3_d4 (N 8, T 18; gate g2.1, 5 flips), frozen here as
    inst/rd3_d4.json (sha1 10a37f90…).
  - All hand-built designs (LEAP, RS3, FPORT, R218 T1-T4) have only case (i), which never releases (L-NEW's LOCK-(i)).

## 2. Lemma FLIP-E (PROVED-CONDITIONAL on [LOC], lane)
Statement: at a state that is not RET for g, with Λ(e_g) = Λ(β_g) = w less relevant than e_g, σ̄(g) = e iff E(e_g) <lex E(β_g).
Moreover E(e_g) = {e_g} ∪ E(y) and E(β_g) = E(x′) (E(β_g) = ∅ if β_g → u).
Proof.
- No tie: equal values give equal Π, so e_g ∈ Π(β_g). e_g's only in-edge is from g, so the β-play passes g and τ(g) = e, which is RET.
  Excluded. So [LOC] applies: σ̄(g) is the ⊴-smaller face.
- TOP-DEC (b)-(c) (non-RET): z := top((A′ ∪ B′) ∩ U_w) decides, it exists (a* = top(A′) ⊵ e_g), and it is an e-vertex (TOP-DEC (a)).
  A′ and B′ are the pre-merge parts and the suffixes coincide. So the e-vertices of the symmetric difference of Π(e_g) and Π(β_g) are
  exactly E(e_g) Δ E(β_g), and z is its most relevant element. The face holding z is the ⊴-larger, because z is even.
- CHAIN-DECOMP applied at e_g (its first e is e_g itself, whose successor is y) and at β_g (not an e; successor x′). ∎
DATA (check219.py; unit: (state, gate) pairs meeting the hypothesis; set: 56 runs: L-NEW's 17 box 192 R-bests, R217's 19 instances,
R218's 17 frozen T1-T4 instances, the 3 NOUP climb results of §5; scope: IDEAL-CYC via own.py): 15,633 pairs checked, 0 violations
(out219.txt).

## 3. Lemma GATE-REARM (PROVED-CONDITIONAL on [LOC], lane)
Split the run into maximal intervals J on which the pair (Λ(e_g), Λ(β_g)) is constant. Let ρ_J be the number of maximal RET stretches
inside J. Let H_g be the number of flips inside intervals whose shared cycle node is more relevant than e_g. Then
  F_g ≤ (#J − 1) + Σ over J with equal Λ's below e_g of [2·min(D_E^J(y), D_E^J(x′)) − 1 + 2ρ_J] + H_g,   with #J ≤ 4M + 5.
Proof.
- #J: each of Λ(e_g), Λ(β_g) takes at most 2M + 3 values in rising reward order (DESC-ALL's count). So the pair changes at most 4M + 4
  times. Each change may carry one flip: the (#J − 1) term, i.e. case (i).
- J with Λ(e_g) ≠ Λ(β_g): the rewards differ and are fixed, so the order is fixed (F2), and there is no flip inside J. Also no RET: a RET
  state has the β-play through e_g, so the cycle node is shared.
- J with equal Λ = w below e_g. On RET states σ̄(g) = e (TOP-DEC (d)). So each maximal RET stretch adds at most 2 flips (into and
  out of it): case (ii).
- Off RET, FLIP-E: the face is decided by E(e_g) vs E(x′). Both are lex-nondecreasing on J (E-MON at y and at x′, whose Λ equals w by
  CHAIN-DECOMP). A flip e → β needs E(e_g), hence E(y), to rise strictly; a flip β → e needs E(x′) to rise strictly. Flips alternate,
  so off RET there are at most 2·min(D_E^J(y), D_E^J(x′)) − 1 flips: case (iii).
- J with shared w above e_g: counted by H_g. ∎
Reading of the three cases (one re-arm = two consecutive flips):
- (i) a cycle-node change: polynomially many in all, PROVED.
- (ii) a RET stretch. For a SET-type gate (x′ = x), RET ⇔ σ(x) = (x, k) and τ(g) = e. Then ρ_g ≤ the number of adoptions of (x, k)
  ≤ D(g) (SWITCH-IN-DV): a value count again. No polynomial bound is proved.
- (iii) an e-set overtake: E(y) and E(x′) must each rise strictly, alternately. By PLAY-BLIND each rise at step t + 1 needs a mover
  that switches at t + 1 on the play at t.
DATA (check219.py, which computes the bound exactly as stated; same 56 runs): F_g within the bound at every gate (0 over); ties 0;
H_g states 0 (no shared cycle node above e_g ever). Max ρ_g at the max-flip gate: 5 (rd2_d9, SW_k8s16). LEAP_k64: 128 flips, bound
131, ρ 0, so the bound is tight up to the interval terms.
- (A first version of the checker used Σ_J (2·min + 1) + 2ρ instead of the stated bound. It omitted the boundaries of
  unequal-Λ intervals, and NOUPC_3's gate (12, 0) exceeded it: 4 flips, faces b e b e b…, 5 intervals of which 3 unequal. The
  lemma's bound there is 6. Only the corrected check is reported.)

## 4. The cost of case (iii), by ascents (labels per case)
- (iii-0) No ascent on the plays of y and x′ during J: D_E ≤ 1 + M(M + 1)/2 (DESC-POLY, L-NEW; PROVED, lane). Hence ≤ M² + M + 1 flips
  of type (iii) in J. This is the R218 trilemma's branch «down»: hidden routes that descend.
- (iii-A) At most A ascents per chain: D_E ≤ f_A(M), where CONJECTURE ASC-POLY(A) says f_A(M) is polynomial. L-NEW: RECUR(A) ⇒
  f_A(M) ≤ (M² + M + 1)^(A+1) (the implication PROVED, RECUR a CONJECTURE with exact data).
- (iii-∞) Unboundedly many ascents: OPEN. What is proved about it:
  - each ascent uses a strictly upward Ξ-edge (UP-JUMP, L-NEW);
  - every declined gate on an ascent is locked while its case configuration holds (LOCK-(i), L-NEW; LOCK-(ii/iii), §6);
  - releasing a lock needs a mover switch on one of its two plays (PLAY-BLIND).
  What is a READING only (R218 §4, §6; hub designs, not all genomes):
  - climbs through the real chain leak (E-DOM-CHOICE at the set bit's own SET gate);
  - climbs through mirror chains cycle (E-CYCLE; T3);
  - an H1 hub reachable from open bits closes Even-won cycles (H1-CYCLE; T4).
- So the case that stays open is (iii-∞) together with (ii) at SET-type gates. Both are the counter question: a superpolynomial F_g
  needs either a bit that sets superpolynomially often, or chains with unboundedly many ascents.

## 5. Corollary GATE-POLY and what f is (PROVED-CONDITIONAL on [LOC]; f DEAD as a function of d)
- If at every state the plays of y and x′ are descending (no ascent), then F_g ≤ (4M + 5)(M² + M + 1) + (4M + 4) + 2ρ_g + H_g. So
  f(M) = (4M + 5)(M² + M + 1) + 4M + 4 = O(M³).
- NOUP genomes (no strictly upward Ξ-edge; Ξ-cycles allowed) are descending everywhere (NOUP, L-NEW). There
  R ≤ Σ_g F_g ≤ M·f(M) + 2·Σ_g ρ_g + Σ_g H_g. With R118 T-CYC and CYC-COUNT, N5′-xi on Γ3 of a NOUP genome makes polynomially many
  iterations before its first fallback IF Σ_g ρ_g and Σ_g H_g are polynomial. Both are OPEN in general; both are small in all data.
  PROVED-CONDITIONAL on [LOC], [1E-N3] and REACH3-CYC's premises (R118).
  - This replaces L-NEW's chain «IN-BOUND, OVT-DV, R-SPLIT, Σ L_g» by «Σ ρ_g, Σ H_g»: by FLIP-E only e-set values matter, so D_E (which
    DESC-POLY bounds) suffices, not the full value count D.
- DATA (noupdata.py, noupclimb.py; unit: flips at one gate, steps; scope: IDEAL-CYC, own.py):
  - 300 random NOUP genomes (L-NEW's rgen_noup, d 1-6, seed 7; noup_7.txt): T ≤ 6, flips at one gate ≤ 3, N ≤ 24.
  - Three 3000-iteration NOUP-preserving climbs scored by max flips at one gate (seeds 1-3; d 4 / 6 / 8): 5 at N 12 (T 12),
    9 at N 18 (T 16), 4 at N 16 (T 10). Frozen: inst/NOUPC_{1,2,3}.json (0 upward Ξ-edges each; sha1 in inst/SHA1SUMS).
  - Four routes for NOUPC_1-3: own = idealcyc; subrun191 C 5 / 9 / 4, R 1 / 1 / 0; Γ3 regions = Oink = n5v xi = n5bxi, verify 0 / 0;
    xi 14 / 17 / 13 (spec 5 / 5 / 4); file noupc_xcheck.txt.
  - Max ρ_g at the worst gate: 0 / 2 / 0. This is weak search, not a bound.
- f cannot depend on d alone. DEAD «F_g ≤ f(d)»: LEAP(k), d = 2, F_g = 2k (R217 §3; LEAP_k64, 128 flips, sha1 b09b2745…). The
  dependence on M is necessary up to the degree: LEAP has F_g ≈ M/2.

## 6. (a) ASC-GATE cases (ii) and (iii) as re-arming routes
Source read: L-NEW box 193 §4 (ASC-GATE: at a gate h strictly inside an ascent a → b where Odd takes β, (i) Λ(e_h) ≠ w with a larger
reward, or (ii) b lies on the play from e_h, or (iii) e_h's pre-merge part has an e above b) and Addendum A1.4 (LOCK-(i)).
**Lemma LOCK-(ii/iii) (PROVED-CONDITIONAL on [LOC], lane; the (iii′) part also on premise (P) below).** At a state s, let h be a gate with Λ_s(e_h) = Λ_s(β_h) = w, not RET. Let b
be an e-vertex on the β-play of h such that the β-play has no e-vertex before b. If (ii′) b lies on the e-play of h, or (iii′) the e-play
of h has an e-vertex more relevant than b before it meets the β-play, then σ̄_s(h) = β.
Proof.
- (ii′): both plays pass b, so they merge at or before b. The β-side pre-merge part lies before b and has no e. The e-side pre-merge
  part contains e_h (non-RET: e_h is not on the β-play), and e_h ≠ b. E-DOM-CHOICE (ε := e_h; w is below every e, as in the ASC-GATE
  setting) gives Ω(e_h) ▷ Ω(β_h). By [LOC], σ̄(h) = β.
- (iii′) needs one more premise, (P): the β-play of h has no e-vertex more relevant than c before the merge, where c is the e above b
  on the e-side. Under (P), the most relevant element of E(e_h) Δ E(β_h) lies on the e-side (it is c or above it). By FLIP-E,
  σ̄(h) = β. Without (P), an e above c could occur on the β-play after b (b's own tail may ascend again), and the lemma does not apply.
  DATA: in every run checked, ASC-GATE's case split never fails (asc219.py: FAIL 0). (P) itself is not counted separately.
- So (ii) is a lock (PROVED-CONDITIONAL on [LOC]). (iii) is a lock under the stated extra premise. Releasing either needs the
  configuration to break at the next state. By PLAY-BLIND that needs a mover switch on one of the two plays; then the face changes by an
  e-set overtake (FLIP-E): GATE-REARM case (iii). ∎
Does the trilemma exclude (ii) / (iii)? No. The trilemma (R218 §4) is about hub chains: where an open bit's hidden route goes. (ii) and
(iii) concern any ascent of any genome. Their releases are e-set overtakes, the open case (iii-∞) when ascents are unbounded and
(iii-0) / (iii-A) otherwise. So (ii) / (iii) are re-arming routes the trilemma allows; whether they can be repeated superpolynomially
often is the same open question.
