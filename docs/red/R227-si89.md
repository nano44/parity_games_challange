## 2. §3's tie statement
- Box 88's Lemma CARRY-TIE: «on steps with constant cycle nodes, Π_r(val_(t+1)) is determined by σ_t, the Π_r-values at t and the
  choices made at Π_r-tied movers». The tied choices are an INPUT to Π_r at t + 1.
  - At a mover m tied at t between g1 and g2: Π_r(val_t(g1)) = Π_r(val_t(g2)). That is the one-step «changes nothing».
  - At t + 1, though, Π_r(val_(t+1)(m)) = Π_r(val_(t+1)(g_chosen)), and g1, g2 can differ at t + 1 because their targets moved.
- Box 88's own DATA shows the effect: the low-blind run, where tie decisions are made without the low data, «stops at once (T = 0)». So
  tied choices DO steer the dynamics at ranks ≥ r, which is exactly the carry channel CARRY-TIE describes.
- Therefore §3's «a tied switch changes nothing at ranks ≥ r by definition; it must change the set seen one level down only» is true only
  at the deciding values. Read as «a tie-decided switch cannot later change the High», it contradicts CARRY-TIE.
- The design step it blocks («a tie-decided switch at l + 2 waits for the race at l + 1 AND then changes the High») is NOT excluded by
  that sentence. Box 89 labels §3 READING, which is correct. The parenthetical should be weakened to the one-step form.
- No construction is offered here either: the open design step stays open.
