**Lemma CLASS-PAYS.** If g flips at t from face L to face R (L, R = e_g, β_g in some order), then class(L) changes at t.
Proof. Write x = Ω_(t−1)(L), y = Ω_t(L), m = Ω_t(R). LOC-GATE (strict, by NO-TIE) at t − 1 and t, with F1 for R, gives
x ◁ Ω_(t−1)(R) ⊴ m ◁ y. Suppose class(L) does not change: Λ(x) = Λ(y) =: λ and E(x) = E(y).
(i) Λ(m) = λ. Otherwise x ◁ m and m ◁ y are both decided by the rewards of Λ(m) and λ, in opposite directions.
(ii) Let z be the most relevant vertex of Π(x) Δ Π(y), if any. It is not an e-vertex (E(x) = E(y)), not c_W (in no Π), and not K0 / K3
  (with the same Λ both plays pass them or neither). By E-DOM, z is less relevant than every e-vertex.
(iii) Π(m) agrees with Π(x) above z. Otherwise the most relevant w ∈ Π(x) Δ Π(m) lies above z, is also the most relevant vertex of
  Π(y) Δ Π(m) on the same side, and decides x vs m and y vs m alike. That contradicts x ◁ m ◁ y. So E(m) = E(x).
(iv) λ ≠ c_W, since one of L, R is β_g and β_g is not a B-ONLY exception. So λ is below every e-vertex (B-ONLY), and e_g lies in the
  E-set of e_g whenever e_g's cycle node is λ.
- Relock (L = e_g, R = β_g): e_g ∈ E(x) = E(m) = E_t(β_g). So β_g's play at t passes e_g, hence passes g → e_g, and σ̄_t(g) = e_g. That
  contradicts σ̄_t(g) = β_g.
- Unlock (L = β_g, R = e_g): e_g ∈ E_t(e_g) = E(m) = E(x) = E_(t−1)(β_g). So σ̄_(t−1)(g) = e_g, contradicting σ̄_(t−1)(g) = β_g. ∎
**Corollary GATE-FLIP-MIN.** relocks(g) ≤ cc(e_g) and unlocks(g) ≤ cc(β_g), where cc(v) = #steps at which class(v) changes
= D^tot(v) − 1 (classes never repeat: R118 (e), R121 E-MON). Flips alternate, so
flips(g) ≤ 2·min(D^tot(e_g) − 1, D^tot(β_g) − 1) + 1. ∎
- CLASS-PAYS implies GATE-CLASS (it names the face that pays) and L-SI's REL-G-type statements on IDEAL-CYC (R229-box93 §1.3). It does
  not use [E-LEV], k or any lane premise beyond F1.
