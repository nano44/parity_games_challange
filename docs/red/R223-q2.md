  landed). On every run of IDEAL-CYC(G), with Howard best switch keeping a best current option:

  found.
- Per e-set-constant interval of ONE mover's own vertex set, there is no LOCAL bound from QUIET: loud steps elsewhere count. A local
  family would still be bounded by (c), so it cannot beat Q1.

## 1. Definitions (as in P-N5-QUIET §4 and R223-flip §2)
- class_t(v) := (Λ_t(v), E_t(v)), with E_t(v) the e-vertices of Π_t(v). A step t → t + 1 is LOUD if some vertex changes class, else
  QUIET. Q := the number of loud steps.
- A switch of mover x at t (σ_t(x) = o1 ≠ o2 = σ_(t+1)(x)) is E-DECIDED if Ω_t(g_o1) and Ω_t(g_o2) differ in class, else P-DECIDED: the
  same Λ and E, and the comparison is made by the β-part of Π or by the length.

## 2. Lemma P-QUIET: proof (PROVED-CONDITIONAL on QUIET; the other premises are P-N5-QUIET's)
Premises: [VJ] valuation identities, DOM (x's value is x ⊕ its chosen gate's value), CN2 (movers and gates are in no Π, so ⊕ at x or g
changes neither Λ nor E), Howard best switch, T-QUIET's (F-cls). All are in P-N5-QUIET §1-§4.
- (a) Let t → t + 1 be quiet and let x switch from o1 to o2.
  - DOM at t: Ω_t(x) = x ⊕ Ω_t(g_o1), so class_t(g_o1) = class_t(x) (CN2).
  - DOM at t + 1: class_(t+1)(x) = class_(t+1)(g_o2).
  - The step is quiet, so class_(t+1)(x) = class_t(x) and class_(t+1)(g_o2) = class_t(g_o2).
  - Hence class_t(g_o1) = class_t(g_o2): the switch is P-decided. By contraposition, an E-decided switch is at a loud step. ∎
- (b) The steps strictly between two consecutive loud steps form one quiet window. By Theorem QUIET it has at most L steps, and a mover
  switches at most once per step. ∎
- (c) Every step switches at most N movers, so the P-decided switches number at most N·T. T-QUIET then gives the bound on T. ∎
- Also, every quiet step's switches are P-decided by (a). By (F-cls), the E-decided ones are charged to loud steps:
  #E-decided ≤ N·Q ≤ N·Σ_v (D^tot_E(v) − 1).

- The design route «non-SET options switching on β-parts / lengths» (R223-flip.md §2, sent to L-SI 45 by the Lead) cannot give more than
