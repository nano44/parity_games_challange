## The obstruction map (L-ALG reading; the fleet's own, unrefereed)
Every candidate in the "certificate + attractor" space pays for nested sibling traps and for counters in one of three coins:
1. memory: prefix-maximum registers (psolC, NE', register games): one register per nesting level, depth = Strahler number,
   up to log n (DJT Prop. 3); cost n^{O(k)} here, (d/2k)^k poly(n) in DJT Thm 32: quasi-polynomial at the frontier;
2. certificates: tangles (tl and variants) handle nesting with O(1) tangles per level but enumerate counter states (tc);
3. time: Zielonka's recursion handles nesting with no memory but re-solves (exponential on T_R, recursiveladder, counter_core).
Hybrids inherit the coin of whichever component is blind on the instance (C3, C4), and the GA finds the composition quickly
when the blind component is recursion. Nothing in this space reaches M4; the quasi-polynomial frontier is the universal-tree one.
ANP's "some other structure of the game" (BARRIERS B3) is still the only pointer.
