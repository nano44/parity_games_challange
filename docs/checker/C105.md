## 5. E-1 (required): REL2-WRISE's leave state s is not restricted to Φ
- L-4 reads: «(Lemma REL2-WRISE, under (A1) … and (A2) …) if (y, z) is adopted at a transition of a frozen stretch Φ at which it had been
  blocked at an earlier state of Φ (a strict release), y leaves z at s and (y, z) is blocked again at q2 > s in Φ, then W_q2(z) ▷ W_{s+1}(z)
  (a special case of RESULTS R91 Lemma LATE-W, which needs neither (A2) nor the frozen stretch)».
- Only q2 is placed «in Φ». s is bound by «y leaves z at s» with no range. Under the global binder, s can be any state of the run, even
  before F.
- The signed statement is narrower. Packet §10 (l. 316-324): «In a frozen stretch Φ … y leaves z at s > t1 … blocked again at q2 > s», so s
  ∈ Φ.
- Both referees accept dropping the order relative to the release: REFEREE l. 226-227 «does not say s is after the release. It is not
  needed»; REFEREE-2 l. 131 «the proof never uses the release». Both readings keep s inside Φ, and neither raised s ∉ Φ.
- Why the wider reading is not covered:
  - The proof uses ARENA-MAX at s (σ_{s+1} optimal in H_s against τ). That needs s → s + 1 to be an S4a step with τ_s = Φ's τ.
  - It also uses R91 W-SUCC on [s + 1, q2], which needs [s + 1, q2] inside one piece (τ constant, no S4c).
  - Neither holds if s precedes a τ-change or a fallback before F. W is not monotone across τ-changes (REFEREE-2's R2-N5-FRAME control
    X_SM_W, behind R89; R89's SUCC-MON is about Ξ, not W).
  - The line's own parenthetical («a special case of RESULTS R91 Lemma LATE-W») is also false under that reading: R91 (f) LATE-W needs the
    leave s → s + 1 inside the piece Q.
  - I have no counterexample instance. The point is the C99 / R92-F16 class: the literal line is wider than what is signed (cf. R92 F16's
    REPEAT′ and REFEREE-2's R92 L-1, both treated as LINE-REQ).
- MOVE: a line revision L-5 = L-4 with exactly one replacement: «y leaves z at s and (y, z) is blocked again at q2 > s in Φ» → «y leaves z
  at a state s of Φ and (y, z) is blocked again at q2 > s in Φ».
  - With q2 ∈ Φ and q2 > s, this puts s, s + 1 and q2 in Φ ⊆ F, inside one piece.
  - It keeps both referees' "release order not needed" reading.
  - It makes the LATE-W parenthetical true.
  - It needs a word-diff from both referees. The draft's entry text (m) already reads «in a frozen stretch Φ, … y leaving z at s», so only
    the title and GAPS UPDATE change.
