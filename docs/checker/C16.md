- Two parts of the clause go beyond R40.

  length". tcL has a constant slack of 3, and R40 §1 says only that the ratio (N − 4)/(N − 2) → 1.
- Second, "S − 3" holds for N ≥ 6 only. R40 §1 says "(N ≥ 6)", and at N = 4 the table has S = 3 with 1 context, which is S − 2.
- Both measurements are of Even phase 0 only (R40 §1 and §9 tables, and check.sh).
- Fix: `first-difference contexts in Even phase 0 are S − 3 (tcL, N ≥ 6) and S − 1 (leanT), both orders, i.e. linear in S and
  within an additive constant of the phase length S (no slack at all on leanT); no refutation of (CTX⁺)`.
- The rest of the pointer is accurate: the families, "(n = 8N)" for leanT, "both orders", the commit and file, and "no refutation
  of (CTX⁺)".
- Optional: tcL has n ≈ 3.4N², so its context count is about √n. "Linear" means linear in S (or in N), and the fix above says so.
