## 1. Proofs (deg ≤ 2; notation P-IDEAL-RED §0: s_0, …, s_T, Mv_t = movers of step t = s_(t−1) → s_t, face_s(x, k) the Ξ-value)
- Facts used, all from P-IDEAL-RED (RESULTS R105) or the model definition:
  - (ENC) Ξ-values are integers; the two options of x have distinct values (each chain contains its own β, and no chain from a
    Ξ-descendant returns to x, since the graph is acyclic).
  - (LOCAL) face_s(x, k) depends only on the states on the chain of (x, k) under s. The key of (x, k) under s depends only on the states
    of the lower-level movers on its W-chain.
  - Step rule at degree 2: x ∈ Mv_t iff, at s_(t−1), the other option o′ is strictly Ξ-better than o = s_(t−1)(x), and under the keys of
    s_t the key of o′ is smaller than the key of o.
- Lemma TRIGGER. Let t ≥ 2, z ∈ Mv_t, o = s_(t−1)(z) and o′ the other option. Define:
  - XiT: [face(o′) > face(o)] differs at s_(t−2) and at s_(t−1).
  - WT: [key(o′) < key(o)] differs under the keys of s_(t−1) and of s_t.
  - Then XiT or WT holds. XiT implies that a mover on a chain of (z, o) or (z, o′) under s_(t−2) is in Mv_(t−1). WT implies that a
    lower-level mover is in Mv_t.
  - Proof. Suppose neither holds. Since z moves at t: o′ is Ξ-better at s_(t−1) and key-smaller under s_t. With no flip, o′ is also
    Ξ-better at s_(t−2) and key-smaller under s_(t−1).
    - If s_(t−2)(z) = o, then z is unstable at s_(t−2) and prefers o′ under the keys of s_(t−1), the new state of step t − 1. So
      s_(t−1)(z) = o′. Contradiction.
    - If s_(t−2)(z) = o′, then z moved o′ → o at t − 1. That needs face_(t−2)(o) > face_(t−2)(o′). Contradiction.
    - The two «implies» clauses are LOCAL: if nothing on the two chains changes, neither value changes; if no mover on the two W-chains
      changes, neither key changes. ∎
- Lemma XIFLIP. moves(z) ≤ 1 + XF_z, with XF_z := #{j ∈ [1, T] : [face(z, 1) > face(z, 0)] differs at s_(j−1) and s_j}.
  - Proof. Take consecutive moves of z at steps a < b: z goes o → o′ at a and o′ → o at b.
  - The better option is o′ at s_(a−1) and o at s_(b−1). So some j ∈ [a, b − 1] is a flip.
  - The ranges [a, b − 1] of consecutive pairs are disjoint. So moves(z) − 1 ≤ XF_z. ∎
  - By LOCAL, XF_z ≤ #{steps at which a Ξ-descendant of z moves}. Together with KEYFLIP: moves(z) ≤ 1 + min(KF_z, XF_z).
