## 1. Lemma CASC-U2 (PROVED, L-NEW)
Statement. In a U2 stretch, inside a W-flat run, an R-vertex of level ℓ := |π_v| switches only at run steps ≤ ℓ − 2.
Proof. The proof of R24's CASC goes through. Only its step 2 (honest values depend only on lower levels) needs a new argument.
1. Levels. The W frame is in route form, so W(v) = π_v ⊕ w on R ((R1), W half). So ℓ is constant on a W-flat run, and a tied
   successor of v has level ℓ(v) − 1.
2. Honest values are route functions with constant terminals.
   - Under ¬H1 the honest cycle C_Ξ passes o, and its top is c_θ ∈ R \ {o}. Every v ∈ R has λ_Ξ(v) = c_θ: v's honest play follows its
     σ-route to o, or meets C_Ξ earlier, and ends on C_Ξ.
   - For v ∈ R \ {o, c_θ}, v is not its own honest cycle node, so (V1) gives Ξ(v) = v ⊕ Ξ(σ(v)).
   - Ξ(c_θ) = (c_θ, ∅, 0) and Ξ(o) = θ are constant on the stretch.
   - Iterating: Ξ(v) = (the σ-path from v to the first vertex of {c_θ, o}) ⊕ (that vertex's constant value). That path is a prefix of
     π_v.
   - So Ξ(v) depends only on σ along π_v, i.e. on levels ≤ ℓ(v), and on nothing that moves. This holds whatever σ̄(o) and the
     vertices of C_Ξ do: their effect enters only through θ, which is constant.
3. The choice rule (R24 TB steps 3-5 with Lemma R; fixes C-1) applies at every switching v ∈ R \ {o}. v is not its own W cycle
   node (c_w ∈ F ∪ {o}), and the keys are σ_{k+1}-routes behind a prefix common to R.
4. R24 CASC step 3 (the induction S_ℓ ≤ S_{ℓ−1} + 1, S_1 = 0) runs verbatim with steps 1-3. □
- Consequence (PROVED [f], L-NEW). In a U2 stretch with f fallbacks and K_W non-fallback transitions that change W on R, the
  transitions that change Ξ, W or σ on R number ≤ f + K_W + (K_W + f + 1)(|R| − 2). This is Theorem U1's steps 3-5. The bound on K_W
  is OPEN (§3).
- DATA: u23check.py checks CASC-U2 at every W-flat switch of every U2 stretch. Results in §4.

## 2. Lemma CASC-U3 (PROVED, L-NEW)
Setting.
- U3, or U23. o lies on its W-cycle C_W = o → A1 → c_w → A2 → o, where A1 is o's W-path to c_w and A2 is c_w's σ-route back to o.
  w = (c_w, ∅, d), with d the W-length of o (R24 Lemma WC step 1).
- KIND: v ∈ R \ {o} is of kind A if c_w ∈ π_v (its σ-route to o passes c_w), and of kind B otherwise. c_w and A1 are kind A; A2 is
  kind B.
- An EVENT is a switch at which the switching vertex changes kind, or a switch of c_w itself. (c_w is its own W cycle node, so its
  choice follows SPEC S1 (v) / FIX 2, not TB.)
- An EVENT-FREE SUB-RUN is a maximal part of a W-flat run with no event. Its steps are numbered 0, 1, ….
Statement. In an event-free sub-run:
- (i) a kind-B vertex v switches only at sub-run steps ≤ |π_v| − 2;
- (ii) a kind-A vertex v of W-level λ (its distance to c_w) switches only at sub-run steps ≤ L_B + λ − 2, where L_B is the largest
  |π| over kind B;
- hence (iii) no R-vertex switches at a sub-run step ≥ 2|R|.
Proof.
1. Kinds are constant on the sub-run. A vertex changes kind only if it, or a vertex on its route, switches across kinds, and each
   such switch is an event.
2. Kind B is closed under σ: the route of a kind-B vertex consists of kind-B vertices.
   - For kind B: W(v) = π_v ⊕ w, so the W-level is |π_v| + d.
   - Honest values: under H1, Ξ(v) = π_v ⊕ θ (route form). Under ¬H1 (U23) they are given by §1 step 2. Either way they depend only on
     σ along π_v, and the terminals are constant.
   - Keys: the W-path of a kind-B z is π_z, then o, then A1 to c_w. Its root-first sequence is (A1 part, o) followed by z's route. That
     prefix is common to all kind-B candidates and does not affect comparisons among them.
3. (i) Induction on |π| over kind B, as in R24 CASC step 3.
   - A tied successor of a kind-B v of route length ℓ has W-level ℓ + d − 1. It is either kind B with route length ℓ − 1, or kind A.
     Choosing a kind-A one is an event, so on the sub-run v's new choice is kind B.
   - Base: for ℓ = 1, the only kind-B candidate is o.
   - Step: from S_{ℓ−1} on, the kind-B candidates of v have constant Ξ and constant key (step 2). If v switched at j′ > j to a kind-B
     z ≠ z1, then z was in the arena at j (the argument of R24 CASC step 3), so key(z) > key(z1), a contradiction.
4. (ii) Kind A, with root c_w.
   - For kind-A v: W(v) = (v's σ-path to c_w) ⊕ (c_w, ∅, 0), so the W-level λ(v) is that path's length.
   - Honest values: Ξ(v) = (the σ-path from v to the first of c_w, or of a constant terminal o or c_θ on the way) ⊕ (its value), by
     (V1).
   - Ξ(c_w) = c_w ⊕ Ξ(σ(c_w)). Here σ(c_w) is the first vertex of A2, which is kind B. So Ξ(c_w) is constant from step L_B − 1 on,
     by (i), because c_w does not switch on the sub-run.
   - Keys of kind-A candidates are their σ_{k+1}-paths to c_w.
   - A tied successor of a kind-A v of W-level λ is kind A with W-level λ − 1, or kind B (an event).
   - From step L_B − 1 on, R24 CASC step 3 runs on kind A with base level 0 = {c_w}: a level-1 kind-A vertex has the single candidate
     c_w. So S^A_λ ≤ L_B − 1 + λ − 1.
5. (iii) L_B ≤ |R| − 1 and λ ≤ |R| − 1, so every switch happens at a step ≤ 2|R| − 4. □
- Consequence (PROVED [f], L-NEW). In a U3 or U23 stretch with f fallbacks, K_W non-fallback W-changes on R and E events, the
  R-changing transitions number ≤ f + K_W + E + (K_W + f + E + 1)(2|R| − 3).
- Where the old frozen-cycle hypothesis (CASC-C) went: A2 re-routings (by kind-B vertices to kind-B targets) and A1 re-routings (by
  kind-A vertices to kind-A targets) are NOT events. They are covered by (i) and (ii). Only kind crossings and c_w's own choice remain.

## 3. Open items (exact)
- (E) The number of events per W-flat run in U3 / U23.
  - A kind crossing B → A at v takes a W-tied successor z whose route runs through c_w and A2, where Ξ(z) = path(z → c_w) ⊕ Ξ(c_w) ▷
    π_{σv} ⊕ θ. A crossing A → B is the reverse.
  - c_w's own switch gains strictly (SG: λ_Ξ(c_w) ≠ c_w) and keeps o on C_W (else τ(o) leaves R, a crossing).
  - No potential is known for either.
- (K_W) The number of W-changes on R in U2 / U3 stretches.
  - In route form, the κ_w-freeze (R24 Theorem U1 step 1) made W move only through route lengths.
  - Outside route form the honest order does not see every κ_w-difference. In U2 the part of a route after c_θ is invisible to Ξ. In
    U3, kinds A and B compare W through different roots. So the CR argument does not transfer as it stands.
  - W is still non-decreasing between fallbacks (R24 TB step 1), so K_W is bounded by the length of a monotone chain of W-values on R,
    and no polynomial bound on that is known.
- Together with R24's reading: (NH') reduces to (X), NFB-O, (E) and (K_W) in U2 / U3.
