### 5.3 The Lead's pressure points (messages of 13:04Z and 13:47Z; the second from L-LIT T69, which I have not read)
- «class (Λ, E)». The box uses the class only through (K1)-(K5). Their genome-only content is [E-DOM] (above an e-vertex there are only
  e-vertices, c_W, K0, K3) and (D1) (the only in-edge of e_g is from g). With these, Odd's choice at a gate is a FUNCTION of the two face
  classes: the face of lower class is taken ((K2) + LOC-GATE + NO-TIE), and at equal classes the e-face ((P4)'s argument: e_g ∈ E(β_g)
  forces β_g's play through g and out by e_g). So no Odd flip without a class change at a face (R126 GATE-CLASS), and no flip at a quiet
  step. This is the step that would fail on a family where Odd's choice depends on the signed part of Π; on genomes it does not.
  DATA: gclass 0, quietflip 0, gflip 0 on the 78 frozen genomes (data/press_frozen.out; up to 162 flip steps in one run and 130 flips of
  one gate) and on every climb candidate. «Odd flips while all face classes stand still» was not found; the closest the climbs came: a
  flip step at which only 3 vertices change class (climbs minch, seeds 12 and 33), and 65 gate flips at stall steps of one run (the
  lowest lagging class stands still, classes above it move; climb stallflips, seed 43, N 23, M 50). Both are allowed by the lemmas.

  ((K3 (b))) and no mover or gate as node (CN2), so its class is (c, ∅) for its node c, one of the M β-vertices: the class of a cycle is
  ONE VERTEX of the cycle, not an aggregate over the cycle (as a mean would be), so no new cycle classes can be forced beyond the M
