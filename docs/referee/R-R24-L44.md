## 3. The choice form (b′): what exactly may be cited
- The lanes' form (R177 §1): at a fallback-free S4a transition j, a FLAT move of h (o = σ_(j−1)(h) → w = σ_j(h), W_j(o) = W_j(w)) with h
  NOT a corner (h is not on the σ_j-play from o or from w, and h ≠ c) has
  - w < o when c is odd, and
  - κ_j(w) <lex κ_j(o) when c is even.
  More generally, w is the index-min (c odd) or κ-min (c even) among h's fully optimal options.
- This is not R24 (b)'s statement. TB's hypothesis is W′(v) = W_k(v) along v's OLD play, and its conclusion compares σ_{k+1}(v) with
  σ_k(v). The choice form compares σ_j(h) with any fully optimal option. It follows from the same steps:
  - TB step 1: W_j = W*_H.
  - Lemma R: the survivors of optimal length are exactly the fully optimal options.
  - TB steps 3-5: the parity flip; the first maximum in list order; the BFS queue order is the lex order of root-first keys, and a
    vertex's tree path is its σ_j-play.
- Bridge B (checked here; the lane's own one-liner): a flat non-corner option o is fully optimal at h.
  - w = σ_j(h), h ≠ c, and h is not on w's play, so W_j(h) = h ⊕ W_j(w) by (V1).
  - h is not on o's play, so h ⊕ W_j(o) is the plain prefix extension. It equals h ⊕ W_j(w) = W_j(h) by flatness.
  - With W_j = W*_H, o is fully optimal. It also lies in the previous BFS layer (ℓ(o) = ℓ(h) − 1).
- CF-1 (REQUIRED of any citing line): cite «R24 (a) Lemma R and R24 (b)'s choice rule (TB proof steps 1, 3-5; fixes §2.3), with bridge
  B», with «its proof uses no 𝒞^1O hypothesis» per L44. Do not cite «R24 (b)» alone for the choice form.
  - B is two lines and class-free. The referees of the citing packet should sign it as part of that packet.
  - «Tied options» must mean the fully optimal ones. A W-tied option whose play passes h is a corner and is outside the rule. 1,371
