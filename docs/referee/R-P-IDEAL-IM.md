- Fact 0: SIGN. β(z, 0) lies only on the chain of (z, 0). With distinct ranks, ENC is injective on sets.
- IM: SIGN.
  - (a) is LOCAL.
  - Rise at y: s′(y) = C_y(s), and the chain of (y, s′(y)) avoids y, so δ > 0.
  - Chains through y keep their prefix before y, so ENC gives the shift δ.
  - (b): both values rise by δ. (c): one value rises and the other is unchanged.
- BIT-CHAIN: SIGN.
  - (ii) at level 1 uses κ^c: option 0 is smaller iff c = 1.
  - At levels ≥ 2 the distinct prefixes decide.
  - (i): the pairs (0, 1), (1, 0) and (k, k) give b_(l−1), 1 − b_(l−1) and the gate bit.
- KEY-DIR: SIGN. x moves iff it is unhappy and its key-preferred option (1 − b_(l−1)) is the other option. The consequence about the
  2(m − 1) bits is correct.
- VALUE-MONO: SIGN.
  - Under ISO, upper chains stay among upper movers or X.
  - Induction over the Ξ-graph gives cv′ ≥ ε + cv(t) = v_u(x, u′(x)), with strict inequality on a move.
- NO-REPEAT: SIGN. Under ISO the upper cv-vector is a function of the upper state, so at most 2^(2m) − 1 strict increases.
- COMPOSE: SIGN.
  - A-sets: chains and the rank order stay inside each part.
  - Keys: H's bits at levels ≤ m′ are K's. G's movers read b_(m′) as their c, and G's gate bits are kept.
- COMPOSE-LB: SIGN WITH GAP IM-G1.
  - The proof gives H's level-0 movers Ξ-target X and a happy start, so they never move and b_0 is constant.
  - It does not say that this constant equals the c of the given flip-free sequence of K. b_0 = [g(0, s(0)) < g(1, s(1))] depends on
    the level-0 gates and on which option is happy.
  - It is achievable: level-0 options have no W-role and no chain passes them (ISO), so their gates and ranks can be chosen freely.
    Choose the ranks so that the started options are the better ones, and the gates so that b_0 = c.
  - Fix: one sentence in a fixes entry.
  - The statement stands; the gap is in the proof text only.
- FRAME: SIGN.
  - (i): Σ(G) satisfies (Φ1) by LOCAL, (Φ2) by IM (a) and (Φ3) by IM (b), (c). Its frame step is KEY-DIR's rule: «C ≠ s(x) and
    C = 1 − b_(l−1)» ⇔ «s(x) = b_(l−1) and C ≠ b_(l−1)». The frame's bit recursion is BIT-CHAIN's.
  - (ii): idle steps are identities and can be deleted; deleting bits never adds changes. Under ISO C depends on the upper state only,
    so Σ(G)'s table is well defined on {0, 1}^(2m).
