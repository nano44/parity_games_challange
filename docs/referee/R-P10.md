## (a) Lemma ENTRY-CHARGE
Setting as in §23q: one context R and one phase, steps σ = (r, τ), and P_i(σ⁺) = the union of the round-r regions of the
tops above τ at visit i. Events (i, q), q = (σ, t): t ∈ F_σ(i) and t ∈ E′, meaning t meets P_{i+1}(σ⁺) without being
contained in it.

Step 1 (event → entry). t ∈ F_σ(i) means t fired in region σ at visit i. A firing tangle has every vertex in CurZ (136-137)
or in CurR (138-139). Both are disjoint from P_i(σ⁺), because CurR = R minus every popped vertex (690, 699) and the earlier
regions are popped. So t ∩ P_i(σ⁺) = ∅. t meets P_{i+1}(σ⁺), so some w ∈ t has w ∉ P_i(σ⁺) and w ∈ P_{i+1}(σ⁺): an entry
(σ, w, i). ✓

Step 2 (fan-out), checked hard. Two events mapped to the same entry (σ, w, i) share i and σ, so they differ in t, and both
tangles lie in F_σ(i). So the fan-out is at most |F_σ(i)|. To bound |F_σ(i)|:
- attractTangle returns true only past 146 (`if (!can_attract_new) return false`). can_attract_new is set at 143 only for a
  vertex with Z[v] false (136-137: `else if (Z[v]) continue`). Here Z = CurZ (700-701).
- Between 143 and the attract loop, only the escape test runs (153-157), which reads Z and G and changes nothing. In the
  loop (165-172) every vertex not in Z is set in Z (170). So at least one vertex is added, and Z = CurZ only grows during the
  step.
- A tangle fires at most once per step: after firing, all its vertices are in CurZ, so 143 can never be reached again for
  it.
- So the distinct fired tangles number at most the vertices added by tangle rules, which is at most |region_i(σ)| − 1, since
  the top enters at 693 and not by a tangle. And region_i(σ) ⊆ R (CurZ ⊆ CurR ∪ {τ} ⊆ R). Hence |F_σ(i)| ≤ |R| − 1 < |R|. ✓
- Therefore Σ_q k_q = #events ≤ (|R| − 1)·N_ent ≤ |R|·N_ent. ✓ This is the step that replaces #fired(R): the count of
  tangles that can fire at one step of one visit is bounded by the region size, whatever #fired(R) is over the phase.
Step 3 (entries vs exits). For fixed (σ, w), the membership of w in P_i(σ⁺), as i runs over the visits, is a 0/1 sequence.
Entries are its 0→1 steps and exits its 1→0 steps, so they alternate and entries ≤ exits + 1. ✓
- Steps: rounds ≤ |V_1| + 1 ≤ |R| + 1 (each non-final round removes a vertex, 748-772); tops are p-vertices of R. So there
  are ≤ (|R| + 1)|R| steps and ≤ |R| vertices w, and N_ent ≤ X_R + (|R| + 1)|R|². ✓
G10-a1 (closed). Step 3 needs the membership sequence defined at EVERY visit, and the same convention must define events
(Step 1) and exits (X_R). §23q names G9-b2's virtual round, which covers only a "no candidates" stop (716, 765). A visit can
also stop with V = ∅ (768-771). Then rounds after the stop have no regions, and the natural convention is P = ∅ there, as in
R2-P7 §3. With "repeat the last round after a no-candidates stop, empty after a V = ∅ stop" fixed once for entries, exits and
events, Steps 1-3 hold as written. The convention changes the counts of X_R and N_ent only by entries and exits at virtual
rounds, and the alternation argument is indifferent to it.

## (b) Corollary C1

## (c) Corollary C2
(EXIT_c): X(σ, w) ≤ c for every step and vertex. Then:
- X_R ≤ c·(|R|+1)|R|·|R| = c(|R|+1)|R|²;
- Σ_q k_q ≤ |R|(c(|R|+1)|R|² + (|R|+1)|R|²) = (c+1)(|R|+1)|R|³;
- F_R ≤ 3(c+1)(|R|+1)|R|³ (R11 (c)).
All ✓. SIGN. (EXIT_c) with c = poly(|R|) is equivalent to (EXITS-POLY), since X(σ, w) ≤ X_R.

## (d) Corollary C3, the honesty clause
- An exit (σ, w, m) means P_m(σ⁺) ⊄ P_{m+1}(σ⁺). By the local PREFIX-MONO-X* (R12 (b), CHARGE, with G23g-1/2 and G9-b2),
  pair m then has a relevant steal at a step before σ, so m is counted in N*_R.
- One pair carries at most #steps·|R| = (|R|+1)|R|² exits. So X_R ≤ N*_R·(|R|+1)|R|². ✓
- With N*_R ≤ Σ_q k_q ≤ |R|(X_R + (|R|+1)|R|²), the three quantities X_R, Σ_q k_q and N*_R are polynomially equivalent. So
  (EXITS-POLY) ⇔ (STEAL-POLY_sum) ⇔ (RELSTEAL).
- (RELSTEAL) alone already gives GAP-A with (CTX⁺) (R10/R11: F_R ≤ 3·N*_R). So C1 is a REFORMULATION of an already-signed
  premise, exactly as §23q says. It is no stronger and no weaker.
- What is new is (C2): the local, FIRED-free sufficient statement (EXIT_c) (one vertex, one step, constant or polynomial c).
  (EXIT_c) for a constant c is STRONGER than (EXITS-POLY), and (EXIT_poly) is equivalent to it.
Confirmed. SIGN.
