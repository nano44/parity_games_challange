- Step 2: under ¬H1, C_Ξ passes o; its top c_θ lies in R \ {o} (every C_Ξ vertex reaches o). Every v ∈ R has λ_Ξ(v) = c_θ: on 𝒞^1O the

  set form: a vertex on its own path prefix would lie on its cycle, where every vertex except c_θ is below c_θ. Iterating along v's
  σ-path to the first of {c_θ, o} (a prefix of π_v) gives Ξ(v) = prefix ⊕ (c_θ, ∅, 0) or prefix ⊕ θ. Both terminals are constant on the
  stretch (c_θ is fixed in the phase; θ by the definition of a stretch). σ̄(o) and the C_Ξ arc after c_θ act only through θ. Correct.

## 2. Lemma CASC-U3 and its Corollary
### 2.1 Setting
- C_W = o → A1 → c_w → A2 → o, w = (c_w, ∅, d) (R24 WC step 1). Kind A ⟺ c_w ∈ π_v; a kind-B route consists of kind-B vertices; a
  kind-A vertex's σ-path reaches c_w before o. c_w is its own W cycle node, so its choice is FIX 2's DFS; making every c_w-switch an
  EVENT removes it from the argument. Correct.
### 2.2 Steps
- Step 1 (kinds constant on an event-free sub-run). Gap U3-2 (CLOSED). The packet's sentence ("a vertex's kind changes only if it, or a
  vertex on its route, switches to a successor of the other kind") leaves open which state "the other kind" refers to when several
  vertices switch at the same transition. Closure: at k+1 every R-vertex's route reaches o (no crossing). Induct along the k+1 routes
  from o: a non-switching x has kind_{k+1}(x) determined by x = c_w or kind_{k+1}(σ_{k+1} x) = kind_k(σ_k x) (induction), so it equals
  kind_k(x); a switching x keeps its kind by event-freeness. DATA: kinds constant on every event-free sub-run (KCONST, 0 failures).
- Step 2 (kind B, (i)). W(v) = π_v ⊕ w (the W-play is π_v, o, A1, c_w; A2 vertices on π_v lie below c_w). W-length |π_v| + d. A tied
  successor is kind B with |π| one less, or kind A (an event), or in F (a crossing). Honest values: π_v ⊕ θ under H1, π_v ⊕ (o, ∅, 0)
  if λ_Ξ(o) = o, §1 step 2 under ¬H1 (U23). Keys: the root-first W-play from c_w starts with o's W-path (A1, possibly re-routed inside
  kind A), common to all kind-B candidates. The CASC induction over |π| with base |π| = 1 (single kind-B candidate o) gives (i).
  Correct.
- Step 3 (kind A, (ii)). W(v) = (σ-path to c_w) ⊕ (c_w, ∅, 0), W-level λ(v). A tied successor is kind A (level λ − 1) or kind B (event).
  Honest values: path to the first of {c_w, c_θ} (it avoids o) ⊕ its value. Ξ(c_w) = c_w ⊕ Ξ(σ c_w), with σ(c_w) the first A2 vertex,
  or o if A2 = ∅.
  - Gap U3-3 (wording). Under U23, c_θ = c_w is possible (both cycles pass o). Then Ξ(c_w) = (c_w, ∅, 0), and the packet's formula is
    false, but the terminal is constant from step 0, which is stronger than what the proof needs.
  - Kind B does not switch from step L_B − 1 on (by (i): kind-B switches are at steps ≤ L_B − 2), and c_w does not switch (event), so
    Ξ(c_w) and every kind-A terminal is constant from T := L_B − 1. Level 1 never switches (single candidate c_w). The induction gives
    S^A_λ ≤ max(S^A_{λ−1}, T) + 1, so S^A_λ ≤ T + λ − 1: switches at steps ≤ T + λ − 2 = L_B + λ − 3. Correct when L_B ≥ 1.
  - Gap U3-1 (statement of (ii); CLOSED by a repair, no counterexample). If no kind-B vertex exists (L_B := max over an empty set; A2 =
    ∅ and every R-vertex routes through c_w), T = L_B − 1 = −1 is not a step; the terminals are constant from step 0, and the induction
    gives S^A_2 ≤ 1, i.e. a level-2 kind-A vertex may switch at step 0. The packet's bound L_B + λ − 3 = λ − 3 says it never does. The
    proof does not show that. Repair: T := max(0, L_B − 1), i.e. (ii) "≤ max(L_B, 1) + λ − 3". Whether a switch at step 0 actually occurs
    in this corner is open in the data: the corner arose in 2 named and 27 random event-free sub-runs, all without any switch, and my climb
    (2,545 candidates from nr_u23_n32) did not produce one.
- Step 4 (iii): with U3-1's repair, kind B switches at steps ≤ |R| − 3 and kind A at steps ≤ max(L_B, 1) + λ − 3 ≤ 2|R| − 5. So no
  switch at a step ≥ 2|R| − 4. Correct.
### 2.3 Corollary
- Event-free sub-runs are separated by K_W, f and the E event transitions: ≤ K_W + f + E + 1 sub-runs, each with ≤ 2|R| − 4 switching
  transitions by (iii); Ξ- and W-changes on R need a switch on R (θ and the constants are fixed on a stretch). Total
  ≤ f + K_W + E + (K_W + f + E + 1)(2|R| − 4). Correct, reading E as the number of transitions that contain at least one event.
### 2.4 Remark (not a gap)
- (iii) is loose. The kinds partition R \ {o}: a kind-A vertex at W-level λ has λ + 1 kind-A vertices on its path (c_w included), and a
  kind-B vertex with |π| = L_B has L_B kind-B vertices on its route. So L_B + λ ≤ |R| − 2, and with U3-1's repair every switch is at a
  step ≤ |R| − 3. The Corollary's factor (2|R| − 4) can be (|R| − 2).
